Deforestation Risk Predictions

Model Estimate Not official government data

Risk scores and future forest cover projections (2026–2030) are generated by a statistical model trained on GFW/UMD Landsat loss rates and DFRS 2015 baselines. They have not been published or endorsed by DFRS, GFW, FAO, or the Government of Nepal. Historical data (2015–2025) is sourced from real GFW and DFRS figures.
